How the numbers work

1 jab · 2 cross · 3 lead hook · 4 rear hook · 5 lead uppercut · 6 rear uppercut. "b" means to the body. Odd numbers are your lead hand, even numbers are your rear hand.

Workout

  • Mount the phone or iPad on a tripod at chest height, 6–8 ft away, screen facing you.
  • Make sure you can see yourself — head to waist, both gloves on screen. You are the target: the targets appear on your own reflection, like punching at a mirror.
  • Gloves up in guard: green on left, yellow on right.
  • Avoid green or yellow shirts, and keep the room bright.
